# Plan: Silver and Gold layers for claude_catalog

## Context
The raw layer is done: `claude_catalog.raw` holds `departments`, `stores`, `customers` and `orders`, 1,000 rows each, with PK/FK constraints. Job `load_raw_layer_claude_catalog` (ID 48588428524370) runs on the Serverless Starter Warehouse (`406e144bbc87fee4`) because no cluster exists.
The goal is a medallion architecture: raw (as received) -> silver (clean, trusted) -> gold (business-ready analytics).

## Silver layer: `claude_catalog.silver` (clean, validated, conformed)
Purpose: one trusted version of each entity. No business aggregation yet.

| Table | What we do |
|---|---|
| silver.departments | Trim text, standardize category names, drop duplicate `department_id`, check `annual_budget >= 0` |
| silver.stores | Trim text, upper-case `state`, validate region against the allowed list, check `open_date` is not in the future |
| silver.customers | Lower-case and trim `email`, standardize `phone` to one format, add `full_name`, add `tenure_days` from `signup_date`, check `preferred_store_id` exists in stores |
| silver.orders | Cast types, normalize `status`, recompute `total_amount = quantity * unit_price` and flag mismatches, add `order_year`, `order_month` and `is_cancelled_or_returned`, check all three FKs |
| silver.dq_rejects | Quarantine table: rows that fail a rule, with `source_table`, `rule_name` and `rejected_at`. Good rows go to silver, bad rows go here. |

Common to all silver tables:
- Audit columns: `_loaded_at` and `_source_table`.
- PK/FK constraints re-declared on silver.
- Idempotent `CREATE OR REPLACE` or `MERGE` loading, the same pattern as the raw job.

Caveat: the raw data is already clean, so `dq_rejects` will probably be empty. If you want to see the quarantine logic work, we can inject some bad rows into the raw data. That is optional.

## Gold layer: `claude_catalog.gold` (business-ready, for dashboards and analysis)
**Star schema:**
- `dim_customer`, `dim_store`, `dim_department`
- `dim_date`, generated from the order-date range with year, quarter, month, week and weekday
- `fact_orders`: one row per order with dimension keys, quantity, amounts, status, and a net-revenue flag that excludes cancelled and returned orders

**Aggregates:**
- `agg_daily_sales`: revenue, orders, average order value (AOV) by day
- `agg_monthly_revenue`: month-over-month growth
- `agg_store_performance`: revenue, orders and AOV by store, region and state, with a rank
- `agg_department_performance`: revenue and units by department and category
- `customer_360`: lifetime value, order count, first and last order, days since last order, and an RFM segment (Champion, Loyal, At-risk, Lost)
- `agg_order_status`: cancellation and return rates by store and department
- `agg_payment_methods`: mix and revenue share by payment method

**Questions this answers:**
1. Which stores, regions and departments drive the most revenue?
2. How is revenue trending month over month?
3. Who are the top customers, and which ones are at risk of churning?
4. Where are cancellation and return rates highest?
5. Does the customer segment (Regular, Premium, VIP) change spend?
6. Which payment methods dominate?
